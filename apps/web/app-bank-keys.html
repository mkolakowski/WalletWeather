Teller and Plaid each need keys from a developer account of your own, so your bank logins count against your own account there. They're checked with the service before they're saved, kept encrypted on your server, and the secret part is never shown again, here or in the app. Step by step: the bank sync guide.

Two-factor sign-in is on, so saving or removing keys takes a fresh code each time.

Your Teller keys

Add your Teller keys to connect a bank through Teller.

  1. In Teller's dashboard, copy your application id from Application Settings (quickstart).
  2. Under Certificates, make a certificate: Teller downloads it as teller.zip. Save it to Files (about certificates).
  3. Pick that zip here, choose the environment, and tap Check and save. The sandbox needs no certificate.

Your Plaid keys

Add your Plaid keys to connect a bank through Plaid.

  1. In Plaid's dashboard, open the Keys page (under Developers).
  2. Copy your client id and the secret for the environment you'll use, and tap Check and save.
